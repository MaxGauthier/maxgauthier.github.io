Research

AI2HI: A Memory System for Artificial Intelligence Agents Modeling User Competence    August 2026

Maxime Gauthier | Undergraduate research project, supervised by Prof. Bang Liu, Université de Montréal

A memory module for AI agents that infers the user's mastery of each concept from their conversations and uses it before each response to decide how much scaffolding to give, so the agent treats a novice and an expert differently.

  • Mechanisms grounded in the cognitive science of learning (zone of proximal development, Dreyfus skill levels, expertise reversal effect).
  • On profiles from novice to expert, the adaptive policy picks the right scaffolding level for every profile, which no fixed policy does.

From Artifact to Assessment: A Survey of Countermeasures to Generative AI for Academic Integrity in Higher Education    October 2026

Maxime Gauthier | ACM Computing Surveys format, not peer-reviewed (yet), supervised by Prof. Esma Aïmeur

A PRISMA-ScR scoping review of 617 studies on countermeasures to AI-enabled academic misconduct, reframing the problem as one of assessment validity: detecting AI authorship does not establish what a student knows.

  • A taxonomy classifying countermeasures by their target: the artifact, the generator, the writing process, the setting, the task or the norms.
  • Shows that the most rigorously evaluated countermeasures verify authenticity rather than competence; one leading detector drops from over 90% detection in its authors' evaluation to 58% in an independent one.

Projects

Le Village: Reward Rules for Pluralistic Alignment    October 2026 - Ongoing

A reinforcement learning study of whose preferences an AI agent ends up serving. A robot in a simulated village picks tasks that help or hurt four voting villagers and Nature, which never votes. I compare standard RLHF with explicit aggregation rules (mean, maximin, Nash, harm constraint) as the minority's share of the feedback varies.

  • Exact KL-regularized soft value iteration, Bradley-Terry reward models (single, EM mixture, per group) and welfare objectives solved through their dual with L-BFGS.
  • Standard RLHF erases the minority when it gives 10% of the feedback or less; maximin and Nash protect it, and only the harm constraint limits damage to Nature.
  • Interactive Pygame interface to compare the rules live.
